# Comprehensive Sector-Specific Financial Analyst Skill

This skill provides institutional financial evaluation frameworks, metrics, and risk multipliers tailored for 12 key industry sectors across Indian and global equities.

---

## 🏦 1. Banking & Financial Services (BFSI / Nifty Bank)

Traditional metrics (EBITDA, Gross Margin) do **NOT** apply to banks:
*   **Net Interest Margin (NIM %)**: $\frac{\text{Net Interest Income}}{\text{Total Earning Assets}}$ ($>3.5\%$ indicates high lending spreads).
*   **Asset Quality (Gross NPA & Net NPA %)**:
    *   $\text{Gross NPA} < 2.5\%$ and $\text{Net NPA} < 0.5\%$: **Pristine Asset Quality**.
    *   $\text{Net NPA} > 2.0\%$: **High Bad Loan Risk** (Apply 15% valuation discount).
*   **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR %)**: $\frac{\text{Provisions}}{\text{Gross NPA}}$ ($>75\%$ represents safe loss cushioning).
*   **CASA Ratio %**: $\frac{\text{CASA Deposits}}{\text{Total Deposits}}$ ($>40\%$ ensures low cost of funds).

## 🧪 2. Pharmaceuticals & Healthcare (Nifty Pharma)

*   **US FDA Audit Status**: Warning Letters / Official Action Indicated (OAI) apply an immediate **Severe Export Risk (-20 pts)**.
*   **ANDA Approval Pipeline**: Number of pending generic/complex drug filings with US FDA.
*   **R&D Intensity**: $\frac{\text{R&D Expense}}{\text{Total Revenue}}$ ($8\% - 12\%$ for innovation-driven pharma).
*   **Formulation vs API Split**: Formulations command higher gross margins ($>60\%$) than raw APIs ($30\% - 40\%$).

---

## 💻 3. IT Services, AI & Software (Nifty IT)

*   **Total Contract Value (TCV) & ACV Deal Wins**: Monitored quarterly in $M USD.
*   **Employee Attrition Rate %**: $<13\%$ optimal ($>20\%$ squeezes operating margins).
*   **Utilization Rate %**: Target $82\% - 86\%$ for optimal workforce monetization.
*   **Dollar-Backed Revenue Growth**: Constant Currency (CC) YoY revenue growth %.

---

## 🔌 4. Semiconductors & Electronics Manufacturing (EMS)

*   **Plant Capacity Utilization %**: Target $>85\%$ to break even on heavy plant depreciation.
*   **Order Backlog Visibility**: Monitored in months of TTM revenue (Ideal: $>6$ months backlog).
*   **PLI Scheme Benefit Allocation**: Ingests government Production Linked Incentive payouts.

---

## 🍔 5. Quick Service Restaurants (QSR / Retail)

*   **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG %)**: Revenue growth of stores open $>12$ months.
*   **Average Daily Sales (ADS per store)**: Revenue velocity per restaurant unit in ₹ Thousands/day.
*   **Store Network Expansion Rate**: Net new store openings per quarter.

---

## 🚗 6. Automobiles & Auto Components (Nifty Auto)

*   **Monthly Volume Growth (Units Sold)**: Tracked via monthly OEM disclosures and Vahan portal registrations.
*   **Average Selling Price (ASP per Vehicle)**: Trend of premiumization (SUV mix vs Entry hatchbacks).
*   **EBITDA per Vehicle (₹)**: Profitability realization per unit sold.
*   **EV Penetration %**: Share of EV 2W/4W sales in total volume mix.

---

## 🏗️ 7. Real Estate & Construction (Nifty Realty / Infra)

*   **Pre-Sales / Booking Value (₹ Cr)**: Advance cash commitments for new launch phases.
*   **Area Sold (Million Sq. Ft.)**: Physical absorption velocity across premium and affordable segments.
*   **Collections & Cash Realization %**: Actual cash collected relative to pre-sales value.
*   **Net Debt to Equity**: Target $<0.5\times$ to avoid debt overhang crashes.

---

## ⚡ 8. Renewable Energy & Power / Utilities (Nifty Energy)

*   **Plant Load Factor (PLF %)**: Capacity utilization for solar ($20\%-25\%$), wind ($25\%-35\%$), and thermal ($65\%-75\%$).
*   **Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) Tenure**: Long-term fixed-tariff PPA contracts (20–25 years).
*   **Counterparty Credit Risk**: Exposure to state DISCOMs (payment delay risk).
*   **Installed Capacity Expansion**: Pipeline of MW/GW commissioning targets.

---

## 🛒 9. FMCG & Consumer Staples (Nifty FMCG)

*   **Underlying Volume Growth (UVG %)**: Real volume growth excluding price hikes (Target $>6\%$).
*   **Rural vs. Urban Growth Mix**: Rural recovery velocity vs urban demand.
*   **Gross Margin Spread**: Raw material inflation impact (Palm oil, Wheat, Packaging).
*   **Direct Distribution Reach**: Number of outlets covered directly (Target $>2$ Million outlets).

---

## ⛏️ 10. Metals & Mining (Nifty Metal)

*   **Blended EBITDA per Ton ($/Ton)**: Spreads over raw material input costs (Iron Ore, Coking Coal).
*   **Global Commodity Benchmarks**: LME Aluminium, Chinese Spot Steel, HRC prices.
*   **Captive Integration %**: % of iron ore and coking coal sourced from captive mines.
*   **Net Debt / EBITDA**: Target $<2.0\times$ during peak commodity cycles.

---

## 🧪 11. Specialty Chemicals & Agrochemicals

*   **CRAMS / Custom Synthesis Pipeline**: Long-term high-margin contract manufacturing order book.
*   **Raw Material Margin Spread**: Price spread relative to crude oil derivatives (Benzene, Phenol).
*   **Export Contribution %**: Revenue share from US, Europe, and Japan.

---

## 🛡️ 12. Defense & Capital Goods (BSE Capital Goods)

*   **Order Book to Sales Ratio**: Target $>3.0\times$ TTM revenue for multi-year visibility.
*   **Indigenization Content %**: Local sourcing percentage under *Make in India* defense mandates.
*   **Execution Velocity**: On-time delivery rate for defense contracts and industrial machinery.
