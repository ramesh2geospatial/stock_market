# ⚖️ Trust, Transparency & Neighborhood Impact

The operational framework governing trust, audit integrity, and community metrics on GeoNagrik.

## 1. Audit & Compliance Framework

To eliminate corruption, vendor harassment, and arbitrary municipal actions, GeoNagrik operates a **Decoupled Compliance Audit Engine**.

### 🔑 Decoupled Token Generation
*   **Mechanism**: Inspections and citations are not logged directly under an inspector's personal name or a vendor's business name on the public ledger. Instead, the platform generates a unique, decoupled cryptographic token per transaction.
*   **Privacy**: This decouples the inspector-vendor identity from public view, preventing retaliatory targeting or bribery, while preserving verification records.
*   **Verification**: Anyone can verify that the token was signed by an authorized municipal key and issued to a valid registered vendor license, without exposing private data.

```
 [Municipal Inspector] ──► [Decoupled Token Engine] ──► [Public Audit Ledger]
                                  │
 [Registered Vendor]   ──► [Crypto Signature Verification]
```

### 📅 Real-Time Slot Availability
*   **No Double-Booking**: The map engine serves as the single source of truth for street vending slots. Slots are locked immediately upon reservation.
*   **Audit Trail**: Any slot cancellation or reallocation must pass a 10-minute hold to prevent backroom exchanges.
*   **Open Access**: Municipal officials and local residents can scan a vendor's QR code on-site to immediately check slot registration and license status in real time.
