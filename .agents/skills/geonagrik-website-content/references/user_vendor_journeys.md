## 2. "For Citizens" Detailed Page Walkthrough

### Section A: Get Started
> "Become an active change-maker in your neighborhood. Map issues, track municipal progress, and connect with certified services."

### Section B: Interface Journey Walkthrough

#### 🖼️ Screenshot Placeholder 1: `citizen_map_view.png`
*   **Caption:** *The GeoNagrik interactive civic map interface showing color-coded pins for local reports and designated vendor zones.*
*   **Walkthrough Copy:**
    1. Open the interactive map to see active civic reports in your neighborhood.
    2. Red pins represent unresolved issues, yellow represents works-in-progress, and green represents completed repairs.
    3. Blue shading indicates designated vendor and market zones.

#### 🖼️ Screenshot Placeholder 2: `citizen_report_issue.png`
*   **Caption:** *Reporting a pothole in under 30 seconds using geotagged photos.*
*   **Walkthrough Copy:**
    1. Click the `+ Report Issue` button on the map.
    2. Upload a photograph of the issue (the app automatically extracts the GPS coordinates).
    3. Select the category (e.g., Road Repair, Garbage pile, Water leak) and add a short description.
    4. Click `Submit` to post it publicly.

#### 🖼️ Screenshot Placeholder 3: `citizen_resolution_tracking.png`
*   **Caption:** *The tracking dashboard showing resolution milestones and audit verification checks.*
*   **Walkthrough Copy:**
    1. View your submitted report on the tracking dashboard.
    2. Track milestones: `Submitted` ➔ `Assigned to Authority` ➔ `Work Started` ➔ `Resolved`.
    3. Once marked resolved, you have 7 days to verify the fix with a thumbs-up or request a reopen.

## 3. "For Vendors" Detailed Page Walkthrough

### Section A: Build a Compliant Business
> "Onboard your business, reserve designated city slots, and secure public trust with transparent compliance audits."

### Section B: Interface Journey Walkthrough

#### 🖼️ Screenshot Placeholder 1: `vendor_onboarding.png`
*   **Caption:** *The vendor registration portal verifying municipal licenses.*
*   **Walkthrough Copy:**
    1. Click `Join as Vendor` and input your basic details.
    2. Upload your trade license or municipality permit (supports Aadhaar/Udyam registration check).
    3. Once verified, your digital vendor certificate is generated.

#### 🖼️ Screenshot Placeholder 2: `vendor_slot_scheduler.png`
*   **Caption:** *Claiming weekly working hours and street slots on the booking calendar.*
*   **Walkthrough Copy:**
    1. Open the slot calendar.
    2. Select your approved municipality zone.
    3. Choose your operating slot times (e.g., Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM).
    4. The system locks your slot to prevent duplicate claims and ensure compliance.

#### 🖼️ Screenshot Placeholder 3: `vendor_compliance_audit.png`
*   **Caption:** *The Decoupled Compliance Audit ledger showing inspection logs and token signatures.*
*   **Walkthrough Copy:**
    1. View inspections logged by municipal officers.
    2. Each inspection generates a decoupled token signature on a tamper-proof public audit ledger.
    3. Review audit comments, safety checks, and compliance scores directly on your vendor dashboard.
