## 1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 👥 For Citizens (Users)

#### Q1: Is GeoNagrik free to use?
*   **A:** Yes. GeoNagrik is a free public utility platform for citizens. You can search maps, check vendor schedules, and report local issues at no cost.

#### Q2: Who resolves the civic issues I report?
*   **A:** GeoNagrik is an engagement platform that bridges public reporting and local government. Reports are routed directly to the verified municipal authorities, local ward councilors, or community-appointed contractors responsible for your area.

#### Q3: What happens if an issue is marked "Resolved" but it isn't fixed?
*   **A:** When an issue is marked resolved, the reporter receives a notification. You have 7 days to verify the resolution. If the fix is incomplete or faulty, you can click `Reopen Issue` and upload new photos to resume tracking.

#### Q4: How is my privacy protected when I report an issue?
*   **A:** Your phone number and personal details are encrypted and never shown publicly on the civic map. Reports are displayed with neighborhood tags (e.g., *“Citizen from Sector-4”*) to maintain anonymity while ensuring local relevance.

---

### 🏪 For Local Vendors & Service Providers

#### Q1: How do I get verified on GeoNagrik?
*   **A:** Register on the platform and upload a valid trade license, vending certificate, or municipal permit. Once our backend verifies the document with the municipal registry, your vendor profile is activated.

#### Q2: Can two vendors book the same slot?
*   **A:** No. GeoNagrik's slot allocator operates on a first-come, first-served reservation queue. Once a slot is claimed, it is locked on the schedule for that period.

#### Q3: What is the decoupled token audit system?
*   **A:** When a municipal inspector checks your slot, they issue a digital audit note. Instead of exposing personal identities, the system issues a decoupled cryptographic token. This prevents retaliatory harassment and maintains a permanent, verifiable public ledger of all compliance checks.

#### Q4: What happens if I miss my scheduled slot?
*   **A:** If you cannot operate during your reserved slot, please release it at least 2 hours in advance. This allows other verified vendors to use the space and keeps slot metrics accurate.
