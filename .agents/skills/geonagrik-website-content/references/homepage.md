# 🏠 GeoNagrik Homepage Copy & Benefits Framework

Professional copywriting framework for https://geonagrik.in.

---

## 1. Hero Section (Homepage Top)

### 📌 Tagline
> **Mapping Voices, Creating Awareness, Inspiring Change.**

### 🎯 Sub-headline & Vision Statement
> Empowering citizens and local vendors through open civic mapping, transparent audit trails, and collaborative neighborhood improvement. GeoNagrik bridges the gap between public demand and municipal solutions by turning street-level voices into actionable, structured data.

### 🔘 Call-to-Action (CTA) Layout
*   **Primary CTA (For Citizens):**
    *   **Button Text:** `Explore Civic Maps`
    *   **Destination:** `/maps` (Open Street Maps integration showing reported issues & zones)
*   **Secondary CTA (For Vendors & Service Providers):**
    *   **Button Text:** `Join as Vendor`
    *   **Destination:** `/register-vendor` (Vendor onboarding & compliance dashboard portal)

## 2. Platform Benefits (Dual-Faceted Grid)

A side-by-side comparative layout showcasing specific value propositions.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│          FOR CITIZENS & USERS           │          FOR LOCAL VENDORS              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 🗺️ Access Civic Maps                    │ 📈 Gain Hyperlocal Visibility            │
│ 🚨 Report Local Issues                  │ 📅 Manage Bookings & Slots              │
│ 📊 Track Resolutions Real-Time          │ ⚖️ Compliance Made Simple               │
│ 🤝 Engage Certified Vendors             │ 📋 Decoupled Transparent Audits         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 👥 For Citizens (Users)

*   **🗺️ Access Civic Maps**
    *   *Copy:* View color-coded neighborhood zones mapping garbage zones, vending areas, and road repairs. Stay informed about what is happening on your street.
*   **🚨 Report Local Issues**
    *   *Copy:* Lodge geotagged reports with photo evidence directly on the map. Categorize issues (sanitation, electricity, potholes) in under 30 seconds.
*   **📊 Track Resolutions Real-Time**
    *   *Copy:* Watch reported issues move from *Lodged* to *Assigned* to *Resolved*. Receive push notifications and verify resolutions with community votes.
*   **🤝 Engage Certified Vendors**
    *   *Copy:* Connect safely with municipality-approved local vendors, verify licenses, and book slots in designated zones.

### 🏪 For Local Vendors & Micro-Entrepreneurs

*   **📈 Gain Hyperlocal Visibility**
    *   *Copy:* Put your business on the map. Let nearby residents discover your services, working hours, and certified specialties.
*   **📅 Manage Bookings & Slots**
    *   *Copy:* Use a smart scheduling calendar. Claim designated street slots and take customer bookings with zero overlap.
*   **⚖️ Compliance Made Simple**
    *   *Copy:* Automatically match municipal bylaws and zoning plans. Reduce friction with local authorities by operating in designated areas.
*   **📋 Decoupled Transparent Audits**
    *   *Copy:* Protect your license. Decoupled token generation logs all audits on a tamper-proof ledger, preventing arbitrary citations.
