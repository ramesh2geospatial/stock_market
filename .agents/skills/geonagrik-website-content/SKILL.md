This skill manages, generates, and structures high-fidelity professional copy and design wireframes for the GeoNagrik civic mapping and engagement platform (https://geonagrik.in).

## ✍️ Content Tone & Layout Guidelines
1. **Tone**: Inclusive, authoritative, civic-minded, and transparent.
2. **Audience**: Dual-faceted (Citizens searching for civic resolution/vendors vs. Vendors seeking compliant commercial mapping).
3. **Persuasion Mechanism**: Highlight the decoupled token-audited slot system to prove fairness and transparency.
